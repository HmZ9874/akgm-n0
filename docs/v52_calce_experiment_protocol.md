# V52 CALCE assigned-rate experiment

This protocol is frozen before downloading or parsing outcome archives. Its purpose is to test whether AKGM-N0 can create a transferable representation and discriminate competing total-effect mechanisms in a real, externally executed battery experiment.

The experiment uses University of Maryland CALCE pouch-cell groups assigned to different state-of-charge windows and discharge rates. Development, validation, and sealed archive assignments are fixed in `experiments/v52_calce_preregistration.json`.

The learner will not receive battery, chemistry, state-of-charge, C-rate, or CALCE labels. It will receive anonymous numeric controls, two registered time coordinates, and normalized responses. Human terminology is added only after the sealed audit.

The primary causal statement is deliberately narrow: an assigned-protocol total effect inside this experiment. Internal temperature was not registered as an observed mediator, so V52 cannot distinguish a direct current-density effect from a temperature-mediated effect. It also cannot claim cross-chemistry universality or a human-unknown law.

The sealed group is `G20_80_2C`. Its archive must not be parsed until the representation and mechanism commitment have been written to the evidence ledger.
